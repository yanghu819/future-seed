\section{Additional Experimental Details}
\paragraph{Base setting.}
All experiments use an RWKV7 backbone with prompt-time Future-Seed only: generation remains left-to-right and causal at decode time. The post-training scripts default to the checkpoint \texttt{assets/weights/rwkv7-g1d-0.1b-20260129-ctx8192.pth}. That checkpoint path is referenced by the scripts but the blob itself is not included in this anonymous repository snapshot. The real-task campaign uses a single NVIDIA RTX 4090 and serial quick-to-medium screening to conserve wall-clock budget.

\paragraph{Post-training search policy.}
The late-stage queues use a two-stage policy: a quick stage for baseline and candidate screening, and a medium stage only for candidates that clear a positive gate. In the final fastdiscover queues, we prune candidates below $-0.5$pp at quick stage and only promote a top candidate above $+0.8$pp to medium budget. The dominant late-stage quick/medium budgets are 140/280 for ARC-like queues, 150/260 for SQuAD-like queues, and 160/260 for MBPP-longctx queues.

\paragraph{Prompt-time Future-Seed variants.}
The post-training audit does not search arbitrary architectures. It mainly compares a small family of prompt-time gates: scalar gates or head-wise gates starting at layer 6, 8, or 10, with variants such as fixed scalar gates, cosine-scheduled scalar gates, and head-wise gates. This narrow search space is deliberate: the goal is to test whether cross-layer state feedback survives repeated queue pressure, not to perform an unrestricted hyperparameter sweep.

\paragraph{Task families.}
The real-task audit covers protein secondary-structure spot labeling, Hotpot- or SQuAD-derived text restoration probes, punctuation restoration, long-context MBPP token-completion probes, ARC multiple-choice token probes, and a small set of constraint and planning proxies. The most stable family under this setup is protein secondary-structure spot labeling.

\paragraph{Probe semantics.}
All real-task numbers in the paper are teacher-forced answer/token-accuracy deltas on task-formatted probes. They are not benchmark end metrics such as EM, F1, or pass@k. A compact task matrix is provided in the supplementary repository snapshot at \texttt{paper/neurips2025/TASK\_MATRIX.md}.
